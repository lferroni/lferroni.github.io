Welcome to the SmartResets blog! I'm Luciano Ferroni a 21 year old student of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Universidad Tecnica Federico Santa Maria in Chile. I had three goals in mind when starting this blog, the first is to share my learning process on various topics related to computer architecture and digital design. The second is making the learning process less confusing for those who want to venture into the topics that the blog covers, even when there's AI nowadays I still find these type of technical blogs helpful when learning new things, so I'm hoping someone finds this useful too. The third and last is to force myself to practice writing and explaining technical concepts, there's a saying that goes "If you can't explain it, you don't understand it well enough" and I think that's true, so I'm hoping this helps me get a deeper understanding of the topics I'm passionate about.
